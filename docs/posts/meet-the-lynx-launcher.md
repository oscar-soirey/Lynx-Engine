Lynx now has a **launcher**. It showed up quietly next to the engine releases, so here is a proper introduction.

## What it is

The launcher is the entry point to Lynx on your machine. Its first version, **Launcher 0.1.0**, was released together with 26.0.2. It also has a **Plugins** tab, which is the catalog fed by the [plugin registry](plugins.html), and an option to enable **Beta Versions** if you want pre-releases such as the current 26.0.x series.

## Launcher 0.1.1 and the updater

A day or so later came **Launcher 0.1.1**, and with it a separate small release called **Lynx Updater**. It contains `LynxUpdater-w-x64.exe`, the updater of the launcher, so the launcher can keep itself up to date.

It is tagged `user-v1` because it is a "user" release: it is meant for the launcher, not for the engine versions.

## Good to know

- Launcher releases have their own tags (`launcher-v0.1.0`, `launcher-v0.1.1`), separate from the engine tags (`v26.0.x`).
- Plugin releases follow their own naming rule, described in [How to publish a plugin on the official registry](tutorials.html?tutorial=how-to-publish-a-plugin-on-the-official-registry).

It is a 0.1 launcher, so expect it to evolve quickly. Feedback is welcome on [GitHub](https://github.com/oscar-soirey/Lynx-Engine/issues).
